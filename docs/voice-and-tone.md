# Voice and tone: one page

Used for the Talking Trees and Map text (SCRUM-72). Words live in `src/i18n/en.js` and `src/i18n/fr.js`. Client rules still win: plain words, never bold, no blame.

## Who is speaking

The woods and its trees, with a kind guide beside them. Warm, calm and patient, like a friendly neighbour on a walk. Never a machine, a form or a manual. Written for a nine-year-old and their grandparent at the same time.

## Five do's

1. Use short sentences and words a nine-year-old knows.
2. Say what happens next, and always offer a next step.
3. Say "we" and "you". Say "That's okay" when something does not work.
4. Start a button with a verb and say exactly what it does. Clarity beats charm.
5. Keep it short. One warm touch is enough.

## Five don'ts

1. Do not blame the visitor ("You denied", "Invalid", "Please check").
2. Do not use jargon: "permission denied", "error", "GPS", "coordinates", "QR code", "unable to".
3. Do not stack exclamation marks. One at most, and rarely.
4. Do not joke at the visitor's expense, and do not be cute when it costs clarity.
5. Do not invent facts, local history or things the client has not said.

## Ten example lines

| Moment | English | French (NEEDS REVIEW BY A FLUENT SPEAKER) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waiting | Looking for where you are… | Nous cherchons où tu es… |
| Success | Here you are. Your marker is on the map. | Voilà ta position. Ton repère est sur la carte. |
| Camera refused | That's okay. You can still meet the trees. Pick one from the list. | Ce n'est pas grave. Tu peux quand même rencontrer les arbres. Choisis-en un dans la liste. |
| Location not found | We cannot find you just now. You can try again in a moment. | Nous ne te trouvons pas pour le moment. Tu peux essayer encore dans un instant. |
| Unknown tree | We could not find that tree. Let's go back to the trees we know. | Nous n'avons pas trouvé cet arbre. Retournons vers les arbres que nous connaissons. |
| 404 page | We cannot find that page. Let's walk back to the Home page. | Nous ne trouvons pas cette page. Retournons à la page d'accueil. |
| Language changed | Now showing the site in French. | Le site est maintenant en français. |
| Audio not recorded | This tree has not found its voice yet. You can read its story below. | Cet arbre n'a pas encore trouvé sa voix. Tu peux lire son histoire plus bas. |
| Button: map | Show where I am | Montrer où je suis |
| Button: audio | Stop the story | Arrêter l'histoire |

The 404 and language-changed lines are examples of the voice only. They are not yet in the site text.
