# Talking Trees recording script (English and French)

Rules for the person recording:

- Use a human-sounding, warm, unhurried voice ([ID 305]). No text-to-speech.
- The words must match the website text exactly (`src/i18n/en.js` and `src/i18n/fr.js`, under `trees.species`). If the website text changes, change this script and re-record.
- All facts are NEEDS VERIFICATION. The French is NEEDS REVIEW BY A FLUENT SPEAKER.
- Record only after both are cleared.
- No Mi'kmaq recording until the client verifies it.

## yellow-birch-1 - English

Target file: `public/audio/trees/yellow-birch-1_en.mp3`

Read in this order:

Hello! I am a yellow birch.

1. My bark is shiny and yellowish bronze, and it peels in thin, curly strips.
2. If you scratch a young twig, it smells like wintergreen.
3. My leaves turn yellow in the fall.
4. My small seeds give birds food in the winter.
5. I can live for well over a hundred years.

## yellow-birch-1 - French

Target file: `public/audio/trees/yellow-birch-1_fr.mp3`

Read in this order:

Bonjour ! Je suis un bouleau jaune.

1. Mon écorce est luisante, d'un jaune bronze, et elle se détache en fines bandes frisées.
2. Si on gratte une jeune brindille, elle sent la gaulthérie.
3. Mes feuilles deviennent jaunes à l'automne.
4. Mes petites graines nourrissent les oiseaux en hiver.
5. Je peux vivre bien plus de cent ans.

## red-maple-1 - English

Target file: `public/audio/trees/red-maple-1_en.mp3`

Read in this order:

Hello! I am a red maple.

1. In early spring I grow red buds and red flowers.
2. My leaves have three to five points, and they turn bright colours in the fall.
3. My seeds have wings, so they spin as they fall.
4. I can grow in wet ground and in dry ground.

## red-maple-1 - French

Target file: `public/audio/trees/red-maple-1_fr.mp3`

Read in this order:

Bonjour ! Je suis un érable rouge.

1. Au début du printemps, j'ai des bourgeons et des fleurs rouges.
2. Mes feuilles ont trois à cinq pointes, et elles prennent de vives couleurs à l'automne.
3. Mes graines ont des ailes, alors elles tournent en tombant.
